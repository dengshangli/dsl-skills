# Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 3, 2026

Wukong Email Template Generator is a skills-only plugin. It processes the email content supplied by the user and writes generated HTML files in the user's local working environment.

The plugin does not provide an MCP server, user account system, analytics service, advertising service, or network integration. It does not independently collect, store, sell, or transmit personal information. Files and prompts remain subject to the privacy and data-handling policies of the ChatGPT or Codex product and environment in which the plugin is used.

Users should avoid placing secrets or unnecessary personal information in email content and are responsible for reviewing generated files before sending or publishing them.

Questions may be submitted through the repository's [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/dengshangli/dsl-skills/issues).
